Our Methodology

The W-Tech Migration Process

A proven 5-phase approach that ensures successful migration with minimal risk and maximum efficiency

1

Discovery & Assessment

Comprehensive analysis of your current environment, including infrastructure mapping, application dependencies, data volumes, and performance baselines. We identify potential risks and define clear success criteria before any migration begins.

2

Strategy & Planning

Development of a detailed migration roadmap with prioritized workloads, timeline milestones, rollback procedures, and resource allocation. Every scenario is planned for, ensuring we're prepared for any contingency.

3

Proof of Concept

Pilot migration of non-critical workloads to validate the approach, test connectivity, and fine-tune processes before full-scale execution. This phase minimizes risk and builds confidence in our methodology.

4

Execution & Validation

Systematic migration of all workloads with real-time monitoring, data integrity checks, and immediate issue resolution. Parallel running ensures zero downtime while we validate every aspect of the migration.

5

Optimization & Handover

Post-migration performance tuning, comprehensive documentation delivery, knowledge transfer to your team, and 30-day hypercare support to ensure long-term success and optimal performance.
